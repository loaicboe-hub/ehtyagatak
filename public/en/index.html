FAQ

What is the minimum investment requirement?

The current standard minimum is 3 greenhouses (360 m² each) with a 35% down payment, governed by admin-configured reference terms.

How do I track greenhouse crops, installments, and profits?

Once contracted and provisioned by our team, you receive private access to the Investor Portal showing your approved greenhouses, crop cycles, installments, profits, and downloadable statements.

Why do operational and financial updates undergo administrative approval?

Mandatory verification ensures complete accuracy before ERP-synced or field-recorded installments, crop updates, and profit distributions become visible to investors.
